### Founder decision: building the section-benchmark suggestions (2026-11-07)

After the section benchmark (docs/reviews/SECTION_BENCHMARK_2026-11-07.md) the founder chose to build the UI suggestions, add to calendar, and who's going with an opt-in per person. None of these has a PDF page; each is recorded here as it lands.

- **Chat (0275):** long-press a message for a reaction row (❤️ 😂 👍 😮 😢 🙏) and "Svar"; swipe a message right to reply with a quote; the Chat tab shows how many chats have unread messages, and opening a chat reads it. Reactions, replies and sent messages last for the session; nothing is delivered (no messaging backend).
- **Map + directions (0276):** after the user pans or pinches the map, a "Søk i dette området" pill under the mode chips limits the pins, the browse list, its count ("N treff i dette området") and Filtre's "Vis N resultater" to the part of the map between the top controls and the browse peek; an empty result offers "Vis alle". The app's own camera moves never offer it, and a "Vis på kartet" link, Posisjon or a Hvor area lifts it. "Veibeskrivelse" next to the address on a place page, in the map's place sheet and at "Hvor vi møtes" opens the route in the phone's maps app (Google Maps on Android, Apple Maps on iOS); only the destination's coordinate leaves the app. On the way the map became north up and flat: the Studio styles carry the camera they were saved with (bearing 13.6°, pitch 14°), which the map took over, and rotate/tilt gestures are now off (there is no compass to undo them).
- **Plans + Varsler (0277):** upcoming plans in Mine planer and Profil's "Neste plan" carry a countdown chip ("I kveld" from 17:00, "I dag", "I morgen", "Om N dager"), counted in calendar days to the experience's next time; no reminder copy. Varsler sits under "I dag" / "Denne uka" / "Tidligere" (PDF p20 draws one list), and a follow notice gets "Følg tilbake" on the card, from the same follow store as Folk (Profil's Følger / Venner counts follow it); a guest is asked to sign in.
- **Create, swipe, hosts (0278):** the first Opplevelse step offers "Start fra en tidligere opplevelse" while it is empty: a short sheet with the experiences you host (published this session, then Profil's "Arrangert") fills in everything but the time, which the host picks again. Sveip shows a one-time card on the first visit ("Slik sveiper du": right likes, left skips, up saves, tap the photo for more) until "Skjønner" or the first swipe; the phone remembers it like the other device settings. The workspace hub opens with "I dag": today's times from Kalender, how many experiences fall in the next 7 days, and how many are on the waitlists of your active listings, each opening where it is handled.
- **Who's going (0279), founder decision "opt-in per person":** like Airbnb, a face shows only for people who chose "Vis meg som deltaker" (a new switch under Personvern > Synlighet, off by default, kept in memory like the other Personvern switches); "N skal" counts everyone going. The Sveip card and the map's browse card read "[faces] 5 skal · 3 plasser igjen" (it replaces "3 plasser igjen · 5/8" and "5/8 påmeldt"), and the experience's join card gets "[faces] 5 skal" above the price. Once you join you count on every surface, and your own face shows only if you opted in. "Se deltakere" names the host and the people who opted in, then "og N andre"; the host's Administrer list names everyone. Which fixture attendees opted in is MOCK.
- **Add to calendar (0280):** "Legg til i kalender" next to "Se alle tider" on the experience page (the chosen time, or the next one) and on every upcoming plan in Mine planer opens the phone's own calendar app with the title, the time, where to meet and the link filled in; the user saves it there. The app asks for no calendar permission and never reads the calendar (the Android calendar permissions are blocked in app.json; iOS has a write-only text for iOS 17+).
