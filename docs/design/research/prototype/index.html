D-13 · D-14 · D-15

Two icon tiers

T1 line icons: 24-pt grid, 1.75 stroke, round caps, outline when idle and filled when selected. Tap to toggle. T2 dimensional objects: one camera and light setup, a matte material, and one Ember touch each. Tap to play the select motion.

What this page is

A visual aid for founder decisions D-01 to D-29 in 12-contract-founder-approval.md. It changes nothing in the app, adds no backend behaviour, and uses no Airbnb or Tinder assets, fonts or colours. Place and event names are fictional demo content.
